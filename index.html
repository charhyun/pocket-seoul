The binder

Single photocards, full sets and sealed albums. Stock is small and real — when a pocket says "1 left", it means one.

How your order travels

Cards bend; albums dent. Here is what happens between your click and your mailbox.

Picked

Your cards and albums are pulled from stock exactly as listed in the shop.

Sleeved

Penny sleeve, then a rigid toploader, then a team bag so nothing slides out.

Boxed

Albums ship in a corner-protected box. Cards travel between two boards, never in a soft envelope.

Tracked

You get a tracking number by email the day it ships.

Shipping to your country

    Questions

    Are your albums official?

    Yes. Albums are bought sealed and shipped unopened. [Replace with where you buy your stock, e.g. the retailer name.]

    Can I choose which photocard comes in my album?

    Sealed albums contain a random card. If you want a specific member or version, buy the single card from the binder instead.

    Do I have to pay customs?

    Depending on your country, you may owe import tax or duties when the parcel arrives. These are set by your government, not by us. United States orders are subject to import duties regardless of order value.

    My card arrived damaged. What now?

    Email us a photo within 7 days of delivery. We'll refund or replace the card, your choice.

    Can you hold items while I save up?

    We hold paid orders for up to 30 days so you can combine them into one shipment and pay shipping once.
